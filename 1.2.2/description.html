A neural network that drives for you.
Hand it the wheel at any time, and take over whenever it needs help.

Quick start

  1. Load any track, and start the run yourself.
  2. Press I or click the AI button in the toolbar for Chauffeur to take over.
  3. While it drives, press the controls to guide it without taking the wheel.
  4. Press I again to take back full control.

The AI button

OffYou are driving.
LoadingPreparing Chauffeur.
ReadyChauffeur enabled and ready to drive.
No routeReady without a racing line; driving on checkpoints alone.

Controls

IToggle Chauffeur. Rebind under Keybinds › Chauffeur.
AIToolbar button. Click to toggle Chauffeur, its color shows the current state.
↔Model selector. Choose DEFAULT for best performance, or RAY-ONLY for tracks without a racing line.
←→Guide it. Steer or press up or down while the AI drives to nudge it.
◉AI vision rays. Show what the AI sees. Settings › Chauffeur.
